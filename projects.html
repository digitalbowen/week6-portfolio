    Mechanics Phenomena Visualized 🪐

    A learning app for kids

    Let's Learn English 🍎

    A picture-and-word matching game for little learners (ages 3–6). Each round shows a picture and four words — tap the right one to move on. Wrong guesses get a gentle red ✗ and you keep trying; the correct word earns a green ✓, and finishing all three brings a confetti celebration.

    A history lesson

    A Timeline of Civilizations 📜

    A side-by-side history timeline drawn strictly to scale — European realms above the line, Chinese dynasties (with their 汉字 names) below. Overlapping periods are stacked into separate lanes so nothing collides, and you can scroll across three thousand years from the Zhou and the Roman Republic to the modern age.
